## What exactly exists before us?
1. Multi-timescale STORES (cascades, fast weights, nested optimizers) — persistence profiles FIXED.
2. Learned WRITE GATES within one store (DeltaNet/DNC/Titans) — no cross-store economics.
3. Systems-level knowledge-lifecycle routing for agents (2608.22215) — heuristic, two media, LLM-pipeline,
   no objective, no theory, no minimal-mechanism evidence.
4. Normative accounts of forgetting/compression (Predictive Forgetting; Anderson-Schooler) — single-store,
   latent-level or behavioral-level, not parameter-stack routing.
5. Formal external-memory coverage theory (2608.11654) — textual events, not gradient learners.
6. [wave 4] Recall-gated plasticity (eLife RP 90793) — biological recall-gated consolidation rule;
   no priced objective, single gate, no engineering instantiation.
7. [wave 4] Expandable Differentiable Dual Memories + orthogonal reg (2511.09871) — dual-buffer CL
   architecture; transfer policy hand-designed per buffer.
8. [wave 4] Freeze Deep Train Shallow (2605.11416) — layer-level allocation heuristics for continued
   pretraining; static, per-layer not per-trace, no online economics.

## Single scientific statement this program claims that none of them can:
> "There exists ONE scalar objective (predictive value minus write+maintenance tariffs under capacity)
> whose per-trace optimum, applied online to any gradient learner, DERIVES its memory organization —
> recovering Adam, exact caching, EWC-style protection, and delta-rule decay as boundary/degenerate
> routings — and whose optimal solutions exhibit measurable signatures (bimodal persistence, transient
> promotion after prediction-error spikes) that fixed-profile mechanisms cannot produce."
